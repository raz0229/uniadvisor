# Grading System and GPA Policy

Grade points per credit hour: A = 4.00, A- = 3.67, B+ = 3.33, B = 3.00, B- = 2.67, C+ = 2.33, C = 2.00, D = 1.00, F = 0.00. The semester GPA is the credit-weighted mean of grade points in that semester; the CGPA covers all semesters.

A student whose CGPA falls below 2.00 at the end of a semester is placed on academic probation. A student on probation for two consecutive semesters may be suspended. Probation is lifted when the CGPA returns to 2.00 or above.

A student may repeat a course in which they earned C+ or lower. When a course is repeated, the new grade replaces the old grade in the CGPA calculation, but both attempts remain on the transcript. A repeated course can improve the grade to a maximum of B+.
