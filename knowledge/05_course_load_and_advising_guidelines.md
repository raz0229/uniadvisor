# Advising Guidelines: Course Load, Semester Offerings and Withdrawal

Courses are offered in either the Fall or the Spring semester only, as shown in the catalog. Students should plan sequences so that prerequisite chains are not delayed by a missed semester. Machine Learning is offered in Spring only, so a student who misses it must wait a full year.

Advisors recommend balanced loads: no more than two mathematics-heavy courses in the same semester, and no more than three technical courses with weekly programming assignments. Students with part-time jobs or a CGPA below 2.50 are advised to take 12 to 15 credit hours.

A student may withdraw from a course up to the end of week 10. A withdrawal appears as W on the transcript and does not affect the CGPA, but the credit hours do not count towards graduation. Withdrawal from all courses requires a leave-of-absence application.
