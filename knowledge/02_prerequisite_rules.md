# Prerequisite and Co-requisite Rules

A student may enroll in a course only after passing all of its listed prerequisites with a grade of C or higher. A grade of D or F in a prerequisite does not satisfy the requirement and the prerequisite must be repeated.

Prerequisites cannot be waived by an advisor. Waiver requests must be submitted in writing to the Head of Department and are granted only in exceptional circumstances, such as transfer credit from a recognised institution.

A co-requisite may be taken in the same semester as the course it supports. Students who are found to be enrolled without meeting prerequisites will be dropped from the course without a refund of the course fee.
