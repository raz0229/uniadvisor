# BS Artificial Intelligence - Degree Requirements

The BS Artificial Intelligence (BSAI) degree requires 130 credit hours over eight semesters. This includes university core courses, mathematics foundations, computing core, AI major courses, electives, and a two-semester Final Year Project.

The mathematics foundation consists of Calculus I, Linear Algebra, and Probability and Statistics. The computing core includes Programming Fundamentals, Data Structures, and Database Systems. The AI major includes Machine Learning, Natural Language Processing, Deep Learning, and Agent Based Modeling.

Students must complete Machine Learning before enrolling in Natural Language Processing or Deep Learning. The Final Year Project may be started only after completing 90 credit hours. To graduate, a student needs a CGPA of at least 2.00 and all required courses passed.
