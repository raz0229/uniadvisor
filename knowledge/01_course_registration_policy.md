# Course Registration Policy

Students must register for courses during the registration window announced by the Registrar before each semester. Late registration is allowed only in the first week of classes and incurs a late fee.

A full-time student must register for a minimum of 12 and a maximum of 18 credit hours per semester. Students with a CGPA of 3.50 or above may apply to the Dean for an overload of up to 21 credit hours. Students on academic probation may register for at most 12 credit hours.

Course registration is not final until the student's academic advisor has approved it. Advisors give recommendations only; the final approval and enrollment action is always completed by a human advisor or the Registrar.
